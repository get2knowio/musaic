# Cover research

A cover result is one recommended image, never a download. Musaic fetches and checks the image itself after the person approves.

## What to submit

- `url`: an `https` address of the image file.
- `sourcePage`: an `https` page where the image is shown with its context (a MusicBrainz release page, the Cover Art Archive, a label page).
- `width` and `height` when you read them from the page.
- `rationale`: why this image is the right one for this release (the edition matches, the artwork is the original front cover).
- `rightsUncertainty`: a plain statement of what you could not establish about the rights.

## Choosing

1. Match the **edition**. A deluxe edition often has different artwork.
2. Prefer the front cover at the highest resolution offered, not a thumbnail or a back cover.
3. Prefer the Cover Art Archive entry attached to the release you identified.
4. Do not recommend an image whose page is not public.

If you cannot find a trustworthy image, submit `no_match` with what you searched. Do not recommend a near match to have something to show.
